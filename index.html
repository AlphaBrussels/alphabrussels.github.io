    About

    Who we are

    A student club for finance and economics in Brussels.

    Alpha Brussels is a student finance and economics club. We run guest talks, skills workshops and competitions, and we publish a newsletter on the week in markets. Our events are also where members, alumni and employers meet.

    Membership is open to all students, whatever you study.
