About us

A finance club that builds the skills first.

Alpha Brussels is a student association focused on finance, economics and investing. We want every student, wherever they study, to understand how financial markets work in practice, and to meet the professionals and academics who work in them.

Central banking, investing, behavioural finance, financial crises: we take the big topics in finance and make them clear, with help from people who work with them.
