Title
Stocks rallied. Nvidia didn’t.
Subtitle
US stocks had their best week since August and Europe hit record highs, even as Nvidia lost its founder-CEO and bitcoin broke below $70,000.
  • US stocks bounced back. The S&P 500 rose 2.5% after the previous week’s sell-off, even though worrying inflation news pushed US borrowing costs up every day.
  • Nvidia lost its founder and boss. Jensen Huang resigned on Thursday and the stock fell 12.7% over the week, as investors moved money to rival chipmakers AMD and Broadcom.
  • Europe hit record highs. Europe’s main index, the STOXX 600, and Brussels’ BEL 20 both ended the week at their highest level ever.
  • Next week: Nvidia publishes its quarterly results on Tuesday, its first without Huang.
