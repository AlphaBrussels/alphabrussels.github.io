## Why prices moved

**A rebound from a rough patch.** Monday’s +2.4% S&P 500 bounce followed a sell-off on 5–6 November, and the VIX fell 22% in a single session.[@glassdesk] Falling oil helped: crude drifted lower for a second week as the US blockade of Iran eased fears of renewed fighting.[@reuters-oil]

**Hot inflation, higher yields.** October CPI rose 0.4% (core 0.3%, a third straight month), and wholesale prices ran 6.5% above last year.[@yardeni-week-ahead] Fed Governor Waller said a core print near 0.3% could put a 50 bp December hike on the table, and the 10-year yield climbed every session.[@glassdesk] Traders priced roughly an 89% chance of a 25 bp December hike, per CME FedWatch on Thursday.[@yahoo-zacks-nov13] That weighed on small caps and real estate.[@investomine]

**Nvidia loses its CEO.** The board opened a probe into Jensen Huang on 8 November, and he resigned Thursday after reports of extramarital affairs. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO.[@cnbc-huang-resigns] The stock fell as much as 14% intraday, closed Thursday down 4.3%, and ended Friday below a $4 trillion market value for the first time since July 2025.[@cnbc-nvda-4t] Money rotated to rivals: AMD drew an Oppenheimer upgrade, and Broadcom benefited from cloud giants building their own AI chips.[@statementdog]

**Europe shrugged off the chip rout.** The STOXX 600 gained 2.4% to a record, helped by strong bank earnings and industrial orders.[@investomine] Insurers, oil majors and names like Burberry and Munich Re offset the chip sell-off,[@mlq-europe] and European equity funds took in $35 billion in the week to 11 November.[@investomine] Brussels’ defensive BEL 20 notched its 34th record close of the year on Friday, led by KBC.[@lalibre-bel20]

**Crypto took the hit.** Bitcoin fell through its August low below $68,000 on Friday, its weakest since mid-August, as rate-hike bets, forced selling (over $1.3 billion liquidated) and Nvidia’s turmoil collided.[@cryptoticker][@cryptodesk] Spot bitcoin ETFs had logged five straight sessions of outflows (about $1.44 billion) through 11 November.[@kucoin-daily]

## Other news that mattered

- **Midterms reshape the policy outlook.** Democrats won both chambers of Congress; Elizabeth Warren is set to chair Senate Banking, which could change the path of US crypto legislation in 2027.[@calebbrown] Analysts also flag possible data-center regulation.[@seekingalpha-week-ahead]
- **Huang summoned to testify.** Lawmakers have called on the former Nvidia chief to appear before Congress.[@cnn-huang-testify]
- **Alphabet widens its AI-chip push.** It announced a broader range of AI chips this week, positioning itself as a challenger to Nvidia.[@taipeitimes-amd-avgo]
- **Euro-area growth holds steady.** GDP grew 0.4% on the quarter (1.3% on the year), but employment was flat.[@investomine]
- **Middle East still a supply risk.** Shipping attacks continue even as oil eases, and the Strait of Hormuz remains the key chokepoint.[@reuters-oil]
