Why prices moved

A rebound from a rough patch. Monday’s +2.4% S&P 500 bounce followed a sell-off on 5–6 November, and the VIX fell 22% in a single session.27 Falling oil helped: crude drifted lower for a second week as the US blockade of Iran eased fears of renewed fighting.28

Hot inflation, higher yields. October CPI rose 0.4% (core 0.3%, a third straight month), and wholesale prices ran 6.5% above last year.29 Fed Governor Waller said a core print near 0.3% could put a 50 bp December hike on the table, and the 10-year yield climbed every session.27 Traders priced roughly an 89% chance of a 25 bp December hike, per CME FedWatch on Thursday.30 That weighed on small caps and real estate.31

Nvidia loses its CEO. The board opened a probe into Jensen Huang on 8 November, and he resigned Thursday after reports of extramarital affairs. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO.22 The stock fell as much as 14% intraday, closed Thursday down 4.3%, and ended Friday below a $4 trillion market value for the first time since July 2025.20 Money rotated to rivals: AMD drew an Oppenheimer upgrade, and Broadcom benefited from cloud giants building their own AI chips.32

Europe shrugged off the chip rout. The STOXX 600 gained 2.4% to a record, helped by strong bank earnings and industrial orders.31 Insurers, oil majors and names like Burberry and Munich Re offset the chip sell-off,18 and European equity funds took in $35 billion in the week to 11 November.31 Brussels’ defensive BEL 20 notched its 34th record close of the year on Friday, led by KBC.15

Crypto took the hit. Bitcoin fell through its August low below $68,000 on Friday, its weakest since mid-August, as rate-hike bets, forced selling (over $1.3 billion liquidated) and Nvidia’s turmoil collided.33,34 Spot bitcoin ETFs had logged five straight sessions of outflows (about $1.44 billion) through 11 November.35

 
Other news that mattered
 
Midterms reshape the policy outlook. Democrats won both chambers of Congress; Elizabeth Warren is set to chair Senate Banking, which could change the path of US crypto legislation in 2027.36 Analysts also flag possible data-center regulation.37
 
Huang summoned to testify. Lawmakers have called on the former Nvidia chief to appear before Congress.38
 
Alphabet widens its AI-chip push. It announced a broader range of AI chips this week, positioning itself as a challenger to Nvidia.39
 
Euro-area growth holds steady. GDP grew 0.4% on the quarter (1.3% on the year), but employment was flat.31
 
Middle East still a supply risk. Shipping attacks continue even as oil eases, and the Strait of Hormuz remains the key chokepoint.28
