Title
AI worries hit markets worldwide
Subtitle
Wall Street had its worst week since April 2025 as worries about AI hit chip stocks around the world, even after Nvidia beat forecasts and Anthropic’s record debut.
  • Stocks had a rough week. The S&P 500 fell 8.6%, its worst week since April 2025, and is now in a “correction”: more than 10% below its record high in October.
  • Nvidia beat forecasts but still fell. Its sales of $115.84 billion topped the $109.11 billion analysts expected, yet its shares dropped on every day of the week.
  • The selling went global. Europe’s STOXX 600 had its worst week since March 2025, and Taiwan’s stock market its worst in more than three decades.
  • A record debut did not lift the mood. The AI company Anthropic raised $81.3 billion, the biggest stock market debut on record, but it failed to bring back confidence in AI shares.
  • Next week: new US figures on economic growth and spending arrive on Wednesday, and US markets close on Thursday for Thanksgiving.
