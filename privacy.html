Privacy & cookies

Your data, in plain words.

What we collect, why, who else sees it, and how to get it changed or deleted. Last updated: 20 November 2026.

Who is responsible

This website, the newsletter and membership applications are run by Alpha Brussels, a student finance and economics club based in Brussels, Belgium, represented by its president, Adam Takáč. For anything about your personal data, write to contact.alphabrussels@gmail.com. We answer within one month.

In short: we do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Live prices from TradingView and the Substack sign-up box only load if you allow them. We never sell your data.

What we collect and why

When you visit the website

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., part of Microsoft). To deliver the pages and keep the service secure, GitHub receives your IP address and technical details of your browser, as any web server does. We do not see or use these logs. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a working, secure website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). The website’s fonts and icons are stored on the website itself, so no data goes to Google when a page loads.

If you allow live prices and the sign-up box

The market prices at the top of the page come from TradingView, Inc., and the newsletter sign-up box comes from Substack, Inc. They only load after you click “Allow”. When they load, these services receive your IP address and may set cookies or similar technologies under their own policies. Legal basis: your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and the Belgian rules on cookies). You can withdraw it at any time with the button below or under “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page.

When you subscribe to the newsletter

The newsletter is sent by Substack. When you subscribe, Substack collects your email address and, if you give them, other details, and it shares with us your email address and whether you are subscribed. We use them only to send you the newsletter. Legal basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time with the unsubscribe link in every email. Substack is responsible for its own processing, explained in Substack’s privacy policy.

When you apply to join

Applications are collected with Google Forms (Google). We ask for your name, email address, school, year of study, your CV and why you want to join. Uploading a CV requires signing in with your Google account, which Google handles under its own privacy policy. Only the board reads applications, and we use them only to decide on and manage your membership. Legal basis: steps you ask us to take before becoming a member (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Please do not put sensitive information, such as health or religion, in your CV or answers.

When you email us

We use your email address and message only to answer you. Our mailbox is a Gmail account (Google). Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering questions (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Names on the website and in the newsletter

Board members’ names, roles and, if they agree, photos and contact links appear on the website, and each issue names its author. We only publish them with that person’s agreement, and remove them when they ask or leave the board.

How long we keep it

  • Applications: deleted within six months if the application is not accepted. For members, kept while you are a member and deleted within one year after you leave.
  • Newsletter: your email address stays on the list until you unsubscribe.
  • Emails to us: kept as long as needed to answer and follow up, and at most two years.
  • Your cookie choice: stored in your own browser for six months, then we ask again.

Who else receives it

We share personal data only with the services named above, which store or process it for us or under their own terms: GitHub (hosting), Google (application form and email), Substack (newsletter) and, if you allow it, TradingView (market prices). We never sell personal data or share it for advertising. Some of these companies are based in the United States. Where data leaves the European Economic Area, it is protected by the safeguards the GDPR requires, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses, as described in each company’s own policy.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, limit how we use it, or give it to you in a reusable format, and you can object to how we use it or withdraw your consent at any time. Email contact.alphabrussels@gmail.com. We may ask you to confirm who you are before we act.

If you think we have handled your data wrongly, please tell us first so we can fix it. You also have the right to complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données), Rue de la Presse 35, 1000 Brussels, contact@apd-gba.be, www.dataprotectionauthority.be.

Younger readers

If you are under 13, please ask a parent or guardian before you subscribe or apply.
